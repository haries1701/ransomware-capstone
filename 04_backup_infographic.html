Safe Backup Plan: The 3-2-1 Rule

Protect Sunrise Family Clinic’s important files from ransomware, accidental deletion, and hardware failure.

3

Copies
Keep three copies of every important file.

2

Media / platforms
Use two different storage types, such as cloud and external drive.

1

Offline / immutable
Keep one copy disconnected or unchangeable.

Copy 1: Daily working files on clinic computers and cloud drive.
Copy 2: Automatic encrypted cloud backup with version history.
Copy 3: Weekly encrypted external hard drive, disconnected and locked away.
